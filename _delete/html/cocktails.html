Spirit-Forward STIR

Martini

Classic Gin Martini

Ingredients

  • 2.5 oz gin
  • 0.5 oz dry vermouth
Method
  1. Add gin and vermouth to mixing glass with ice
  2. Stir 30–40 seconds until glass is frosty
  3. Strain into chilled martini glass
🍋 Lemon twist or olives 🥂 Martini or coupe
Dirty → +0.5 oz olive brine  |  Vodka → swap gin

Manhattan

The Classic New York Cocktail

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters
Method
  1. Add all ingredients to mixing glass with ice
  2. Stir 30–40 seconds
  3. Strain into chilled coupe
🍒 Luxardo cherry 🥂 Coupe or Nick & Nora
Rob Roy → Scotch  |  Perfect → half sweet/dry vermouth

Negroni

The Italian Bitter Classic

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
Method
  1. Add all ingredients to mixing glass with ice
  2. Stir 20–30 seconds
  3. Strain over large ice cube in rocks glass
🍊 Orange peel 🥃 Rocks glass
Boulevardier → bourbon  |  Sbagliato → Prosecco

Old Fashioned

The Original Cocktail

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon or rye
  • 0.25 oz simple syrup
  • 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters
Method
  1. Add simple syrup and bitters to rocks glass
  2. Add whiskey and large ice cube
  3. Stir 20–30 seconds
🍊 Orange peel 🥃 Rocks glass

Whiskey Sour

The Perfect Sour Template

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon or rye
  • 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 0.75 oz simple syrup
  • 0.5 oz egg white (optional)
Method
  1. Dry shake (no ice) 10 sec if using egg white
  2. Add ice, shake hard 10–15 seconds
  3. Strain over fresh ice
🍒 Cherry + orange 🥃 Rocks glass
NY Sour → float red wine  |  Boston → with egg white

Long Island Iced Tea

Dangerously Strong, Deceptively Smooth

Ingredients

  • 0.5 oz ea. vodka, rum, gin, tequila, Cointreau
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 0.5 oz simple syrup
  • splash cola
Method
  1. Shake all except cola hard 10–15 seconds
  2. Strain into Collins glass over ice
  3. Top with small splash of cola, stir gently
🍋 Lemon wedge 🥤 Collins glass
⚠️ ~22% ABV — sip responsibly

Sour & Bright BATCH + CHILL

Sangria

Spanish Wine Punch — Serves 4–6

Ingredients

  • 750 ml Spanish red wine
  • 2 oz brandy
  • ¼ cup fresh orange juice
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • — orange, apple, lemon sliced
Method
  1. Combine wine, brandy, OJ, and sugar in pitcher
  2. Add fruit and cinnamon stick
  3. Chill 4–24 hours
  4. Serve over ice, optional club soda top
🍊 Orange slice 🍷 Wine glass

Mojito

Cuba's Refreshing Classic

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 0.75 oz simple syrup
  • 8–10 fresh mint leaves
  • 2–3 oz club soda
Method
  1. Gently muddle mint with simple syrup (don't shred!)
  2. Add rum and lime juice
  3. Fill with ice, top with club soda, stir gently
🌿 Mint sprig + lime 🥤 Collins glass

Bitter & Complex STIR + RINSE

Sazerac

New Orleans' Official Cocktail

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • 0.25 oz simple syrup
  • 3 dashes Peychaud's bitters
  • rinse absinthe
Method
  1. Rinse chilled rocks glass with absinthe, discard excess
  2. Stir rye, syrup, and bitters with ice 30–40 seconds
  3. Strain into glass (no ice)
  4. Express lemon peel over drink, discard peel
🍋 Lemon peel (discarded) 🥃 Rocks glass

Rich & Creamy SHAKE

Espresso Martini

Caffeinated Elegance

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1 oz Kahlúa
  • 1 oz fresh espresso (cooled)
  • 0.25 oz simple syrup
Method
  1. Cool espresso to room temp first
  2. Shake HARD 15–20 seconds (creates foam)
  3. Strain into chilled martini glass
☕ 3 coffee beans 🥂 Martini or coupe

The Golden Ratios

Classic Sour

2 oz spirit
0.75 oz citrus
0.75 oz sweetener

Daiquiri / Margarita

2 oz spirit
1 oz citrus
0.75 oz sweetener

Equal Parts

Negroni: 1:1:1
Last Word: ¾:¾:¾:¾

Martini Range

Classic: 5:1
Dry: 6:1 or rinse
Wet: 2:1
50/50: equal

Adjusting to Taste

Too sweet → more citrus
Too sour → more syrup
Too strong → more mixer
Too bitter → add syrup

These are templates. Once you master them, adjust to your taste.
