# The Bartender's Guide
*Quick reference for technique and classic cocktails*

---

## Bar Essentials

### Core Spirits (The Essential Six)
- **Vodka** → Martinis, Cosmos, Moscow Mules, White Russians
- **Gin** → Martinis, Negronis, G&Ts, Tom Collins
- **White Rum** → Mojitos, Daiquiris, Piña Coladas
- **Tequila (Blanco)** → Margaritas, Palomas (100% agave only)
- **Whiskey** → Old Fashioneds, Manhattans, Whiskey Sours
- **Brandy/Cognac** → Sidecars, Brandy Alexanders

### Key Modifiers
- **Dry Vermouth** → Martinis (refrigerate after opening, use within 1-2 months)
- **Sweet Vermouth** → Manhattans, Negronis (refrigerate)
- **Orange Liqueur** → Margaritas, Sidecars (Cointreau > Triple Sec)
- **Coffee Liqueur** → White Russians, Espresso Martinis (Kahlúa standard)
- **Campari** → Negronis (bitter, acquired taste)
- **Aperol** → Aperol Spritzes (lighter, sweeter than Campari)

### Mixers
- **Fresh Citrus** → Lemons, limes, oranges (NEVER bottled juice)
- **Simple Syrup** → 1:1 sugar:water, dissolved and cooled
- **Club Soda** → Unflavored carbonated water
- **Tonic Water** → Bitter, sweetened (Fever-Tree recommended)
- **Ginger Beer** → NOT ginger ale (spicy, strong flavor)
- **Bitters** → Angostura (aromatic), Orange (citrusy)

### Tools
- **Shaker** → Boston (pro) or Cobbler (beginner-friendly)
- **Jigger** → 1 oz / 2 oz or 0.75 oz / 1.5 oz measuring tool
- **Bar Spoon** → Long-handled for stirring
- **Strainer** → Hawthorne (for shakers) or Julep (for mixing glass)
- **Muddler** → For crushing herbs/fruit
- **Citrus Juicer** → Essential for fresh juice
- **Peeler** → For citrus twists

## Technique Reference

### The Five Fundamentals

**SHAKE** → Citrus, juice, cream, egg whites, syrups  
- Fill shaker 3/4 with ice, seal, shake hard 10-15 seconds
- Sound changes from "crunchy" to "wet" when ready
- Examples: Margarita, Daiquiri, Whiskey Sour, Cosmo

**STIR** → Spirit-only cocktails (no juice/cream/citrus)  
- Add ice to mixing glass, pour spirits, stir 30-40 seconds
- Stir until glass exterior is frosty
- Examples: Martini, Manhattan, Negroni, Old Fashioned

**BUILD** → Simple highballs  
- Ice in glass → spirit → mixer → quick stir
- Examples: G&T, Rum & Coke, Moscow Mule

**MUDDLE** → Extract oils/flavor from herbs or fruit  
- Herbs: Gentle pressure, twist, release oils (don't shred)
- Fruit: More aggressive to break down
- Examples: Mojito, Mint Julep, Old Fashioned (sugar method)

**BLEND** → Frozen drinks  
- Spirits + mixers + ice, blend until smooth
- Use crushed ice if available
- Examples: Frozen Margarita, Piña Colada

### Common Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Neat** | Room temp, no ice, nothing else |
| **On the Rocks** | Served over ice |
| **Up / Straight Up** | Chilled with ice, strained into glass with no ice |
| **Dirty** | Made with olive brine (usually Martinis) |
| **Dry** | Less vermouth |
| **Wet** | More vermouth |
| **With a Back** | With a chaser on the side |
| **Twist** | Citrus peel, twisted to release oils |
| **Expressed** | Citrus oils sprayed over drink |
| **Float** | Ingredient poured gently on top |
| **Dash** | ~1/8 tsp from bitters bottle |
| **Splash** | ~1/4-1/2 oz imprecise pour |

### Measurements
- 1 dash = 1/8 tsp
- 1 barspoon = 1 tsp
- 1 oz = 2 tbsp = 30 ml
- 1 jigger = typically 1 oz / 2 oz sides

**The Golden Sour Ratio:** 2 oz spirit : 0.75 oz citrus : 0.75 oz sweet

| Glass | Size | Best For |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Rocks/Old Fashioned** | 6-10 oz | Old Fashioneds, Negronis, whiskey rocks |
| **Coupe** | 5-7 oz | Daiquiris, Sidecars, shaken cocktails "up" |
| **Martini (V-shape)** | 4-6 oz | Martinis, Cosmos, Lemon Drops |

## The Golden Rules

1. **Fresh citrus juice, always** → No bottled juice, ever
2. **Measure your ingredients** → Use a jigger until you're a pro
3. **Chill your glassware** → Freezer or ice water while mixing
4. **Stir stirred, shake shaken** → Spirit-only = stir; juice/citrus = shake
5. **Taste as you go** → Adjust sweetness, tartness, strength
6. **Simple garnishes** → Must add aroma, flavor, or visual appeal
7. **Make classics yours** → Learn the rules, then adapt to taste

## Classic Cocktails by Category

### SPIRIT-FORWARD (Strong & Boozy)

**MARTINI**  
*2.5 oz gin + 0.5 oz dry vermouth*  
- Stir with ice 30-40s → strain into chilled glass
- Garnish: Lemon twist OR olives
- Variations: Dry (less vermouth), Dirty (+olive brine), Vodka (swap gin), 50/50 (equal parts)

**MANHATTAN**  
*2 oz rye + 1 oz sweet vermouth + 2-3 dashes Angostura*  
- Stir with ice 30-40s → strain into chilled coupe
- Garnish: Cherry + optional orange twist
- Variations: Perfect (half dry/half sweet vermouth), Rob Roy (Scotch)

**NEGRONI**  
*1 oz gin + 1 oz Campari + 1 oz sweet vermouth*  
- Stir 20-30s → strain over large ice cube in rocks glass
- Garnish: Orange peel (expressed)
- Variations: Boulevardier (bourbon), Sbagliato (Prosecco), White Negroni (Lillet + Suze)

**OLD FASHIONED**  
*2 oz bourbon/rye + 0.25 oz simple syrup + 2-3 dashes Angostura*  
- Build in rocks glass: muddle sugar + bitters OR combine all with ice and stir
- Serve over large ice cube
- Garnish: Orange peel (expressed)

---

### SOUR & BRIGHT (Citrus-Forward)

**WHISKEY SOUR**  
*2 oz bourbon/rye + 0.75 oz lemon + 0.75 oz simple syrup*  
- Shake hard 10-15s → strain over ice in rocks glass
- Optional: Add 0.5 oz egg white (dry shake first, then with ice)
- Garnish: Cherry + orange slice
- Variation: New York Sour (float red wine on top)

**MARGARITA**  
*2 oz tequila + 1 oz lime + 0.75 oz Cointreau*  
- Optional: Salt rim (half only)
- Shake hard 10-15s → strain over ice in rocks glass
- Garnish: Lime wedge
- Variations: Tommy's (agave instead of Cointreau), Frozen (blend with ice)

**DAIQUIRI**  
*2 oz white rum + 1 oz lime + 0.75 oz simple syrup*  
- Shake hard 10-15s → strain into chilled coupe
- No garnish needed (simple perfection)

**COSMOPOLITAN**  
*1.5 oz vodka + 1 oz cranberry + 0.5 oz lime + 0.5 oz Cointreau*  
- Shake hard 10-15s → strain into chilled martini glass
- Garnish: Lime twist or orange peel

---

### SWEET & EASY (Crowd-Pleasers)

**PIÑA COLADA**  
*2 oz white rum + 2 oz pineapple juice + 1.5 oz cream of coconut*  
- Blend with 1-1.5 cups ice until smooth OR shake and strain over ice
- Garnish: Pineapple wedge + cherry

**WHITE RUSSIAN**  
*2 oz vodka + 1 oz Kahlúa + 1 oz heavy cream*  
- Build in rocks glass over ice: vodka + Kahlúa, stir
- Float cream on top (or stir in)
- Variation: Black Russian (no cream)

**MAI TAI**  
*2 oz aged rum + 0.75 oz Cointreau + 0.75 oz lime + 0.5 oz orgeat + 0.25 oz simple*  
- Shake hard → strain over crushed ice in rocks glass
- Garnish: Mint sprig + lime wheel

---

### LIGHT & REFRESHING (Low ABV)

**MOJITO**  
*2 oz white rum + 1 oz lime + 0.75 oz simple + 8-10 mint leaves + club soda*  
- Muddle mint + simple gently in glass
- Add rum + lime + ice
- Top with 2-3 oz club soda, stir gently
- Garnish: Mint sprig + lime wedge

**GIN & TONIC**  
*2 oz gin + 4-6 oz tonic water*  
- Build in highball over ice
- Stir gently
- Garnish: Lime wedge (squeezed)

**MOSCOW MULE**  
*2 oz vodka + 0.5 oz lime + 4-6 oz ginger beer*  
- Build in copper mug or highball over ice
- Stir gently
- Garnish: Lime wedge
- Variations: Dark & Stormy (dark rum), Kentucky Mule (bourbon)

**TOM COLLINS**  
*2 oz gin + 1 oz lemon + 0.5 oz simple + club soda*  
- Shake gin + lemon + simple with ice
- Strain into Collins glass over ice
- Top with 2-3 oz club soda
- Garnish: Lemon wheel + cherry

**PALOMA**  
*2 oz tequila + 0.5 oz lime + grapefruit soda (or juice + club soda)*  
- Salt rim optional
- Build in highball over ice
- Stir gently
- Garnish: Grapefruit wedge

---

### BITTER & COMPLEX (Aperitifs)

**APEROL SPRITZ**  
*3 oz Prosecco + 2 oz Aperol + 1 oz club soda*  
- Build in wine glass over ice (ratio 3:2:1)
- Stir gently
- Garnish: Orange slice

**CAMPARI SPRITZ**  
*3 oz Prosecco + 2 oz Campari + 1 oz club soda*  
- Same as Aperol Spritz (more bitter)

**SAZERAC**  
*2 oz rye + 0.25 oz simple + 3 dashes Peychaud's + absinthe rinse*  
- Rinse chilled rocks glass with absinthe, dump excess
- Stir rye + simple + bitters with ice 30-40s
- Strain into absinthe-rinsed glass (no ice)
- Express lemon peel, discard (don't drop in)

---

### RICH & CREAMY (Dessert Drinks)

**ESPRESSO MARTINI**  
*2 oz vodka + 1 oz Kahlúa + 1 oz fresh espresso + 0.25 oz simple*  
- Cool espresso to room temp
- Shake HARD 15-20s (creates foam)
- Strain into chilled martini glass
- Garnish: 3 coffee beans on foam

**BRANDY ALEXANDER**  
*1.5 oz brandy + 1 oz dark crème de cacao + 1 oz heavy cream*  
- Shake hard 10-15s
- Strain into chilled coupe
- Garnish: Grated nutmeg

## Pro Tips

### Ice Matters
- **Standard cubes:** Most drinks
- **Large cubes (2"):** Rocks drinks (slower melt, less dilution)
- **Crushed ice:** Mint Juleps, Tiki drinks (chills fast, melts fast)
- Fill shaker 3/4 full • Fresh ice for each drink • Never reuse

### Citrus
- Fresh juice lasts ~4 hours before oxidizing
- Always squeeze fresh, never use bottled
- Lemon for whiskey drinks, lime for rum/tequila

### Vermouth
- It's wine → refrigerate after opening
- Use within 1-2 months
- Old vermouth = ruined drink

### Garnishes
- **Twist:** Express oils over drink, rub rim, drop in or discard
- **Expressed:** Spray citrus oils over surface
- **Rim:** Citrus wedge + salt/sugar (rim half only to give choice)

### Common Ratios
- **Classic Sour:** 2 : 0.75 : 0.75 (spirit : citrus : sweet)
- **Daiquiri/Margarita family:** 2 : 1 : 0.75
- **Negroni:** 1 : 1 : 1 (equal parts)
- **Martini:** 5:1 to 2:1 (gin : vermouth, adjust to taste)
