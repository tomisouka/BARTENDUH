## SPIRIT-FORWARD (Strong & Boozy)

### 🍸 MARTINI | Spirit-Forward
**Classic Gin Martini**

![Martini](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7c/Martini_with_olive.jpg/320px-Martini_with_olive.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2.5 oz gin
- 0.5 oz dry vermouth

**Method:** STIR
1. Add gin and dry vermouth to mixing glass with ice
2. Stir 30-40 seconds until glass is frosty
3. Strain into chilled martini glass

**Garnish:** Lemon twist OR olives

**Variations:**
- **Dry Martini** → Less vermouth (just a rinse)
- **Wet Martini** → More vermouth (equal parts = 50/50)
- **Dirty Martini** → Add 0.5 oz olive brine
- **Vodka Martini** → Swap gin for vodka

**Glass:** Martini or coupe

---

### 🥃 MANHATTAN | Spirit-Forward
**The Classic New York Cocktail**

![Manhattan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e9/A_Manhattan_Cocktail.jpg/320px-A_Manhattan_Cocktail.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz rye whiskey
- 1 oz sweet vermouth
- 2-3 dashes Angostura bitters

**Method:** STIR
1. Add all ingredients to mixing glass with ice
2. Stir 30-40 seconds
3. Strain into chilled coupe

**Garnish:** Luxardo cherry + optional orange twist

**Variations:**
- **Perfect Manhattan** → Half sweet, half dry vermouth
- **Rob Roy** → Scotch instead of rye
- **Black Manhattan** → Averna instead of sweet vermouth

**Glass:** Coupe or Nick & Nora

---

### 🍊 NEGRONI | Spirit-Forward
**The Italian Bitter Classic**

![Negroni](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Negroni_with_orange_garnish.jpg/320px-Negroni_with_orange_garnish.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 oz gin
- 1 oz Campari
- 1 oz sweet vermouth

**Method:** STIR
1. Add all ingredients to mixing glass with ice
2. Stir 20-30 seconds
3. Strain over large ice cube in rocks glass

**Garnish:** Orange peel (expressed over drink)

**Variations:**
- **Boulevardier** → Bourbon instead of gin
- **Negroni Sbagliato** → Prosecco instead of gin
- **White Negroni** → Lillet Blanc + Suze instead of Campari + vermouth

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned

---

### 🥃 OLD FASHIONED | Spirit-Forward
**The Original Cocktail**

![Old Fashioned](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/84/Old_Fashioned_Cocktail.jpg/320px-Old_Fashioned_Cocktail.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey
- 0.25 oz simple syrup (or 1 sugar cube)
- 2-3 dashes Angostura bitters

**Method:** BUILD or STIR
1. Add simple syrup and bitters to rocks glass
2. Add whiskey and large ice cube
3. Stir 20-30 seconds

**Alternative method (sugar cube):**
1. Muddle sugar cube with bitters and splash of water
2. Add whiskey and large ice cube, stir

**Garnish:** Orange peel (expressed), optional cherry

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned

## SOUR & BRIGHT (Citrus-Forward)

### 🍋 WHISKEY SOUR | Sour & Bright
**The Perfect Sour Template**

![Whiskey Sour](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/thirty-five/Whiskey_sour_2021.jpg/320px-Whiskey_sour_2021.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey
- 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- Optional: 0.5 oz egg white

**Method:** SHAKE
1. If using egg white: dry shake (no ice) 10 seconds
2. Add ice and shake hard 10-15 seconds
3. Strain over fresh ice in rocks glass

**Garnish:** Cherry + orange slice

**Variations:**
- **New York Sour** → Float red wine on top
- **Boston Sour** → With egg white (silky foam)
- **Amaretto Sour** → Amaretto instead of whiskey

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned

### 🍹 MARGARITA | Sour & Bright
**Mexico's Gift to the World**

![Margarita](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/thirty-four/MargaritaReal.jpg/320px-MargaritaReal.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz tequila (100% agave blanco)
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz Cointreau (or triple sec)
- Optional: salt rim

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Optional: rim half the glass with salt (lime wedge + salt)
2. Shake all ingredients hard 10-15 seconds
3. Strain over fresh ice in rocks glass

**Garnish:** Lime wedge

**Variations:**
- **Tommy's Margarita** → 0.5 oz agave nectar instead of Cointreau
- **Frozen Margarita** → Blend with ice until smooth
- **Spicy Margarita** → Muddle jalapeño slices

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned or margarita glass

### 🍹 DAIQUIRI | Sour & Bright
**Cuba's Classic**

![Daiquiri](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5b/Daiquiri_Cocktail.jpg/320px-Daiquiri_Cocktail.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz white rum
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Shake all ingredients hard 10-15 seconds
2. Strain into chilled coupe

**Garnish:** None (simple perfection) or lime wheel

**Variations:**
- **Hemingway Daiquiri** → Add grapefruit juice + maraschino liqueur, no simple syrup
- **Frozen Daiquiri** → Blend with ice
- **Strawberry Daiquiri** → Add fresh strawberries

**Glass:** Coupe

### 🍋 LEMON DROP | Sour & Bright
**Sweet, Tart & Sugared**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz vodka (citrus vodka preferred)
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.5 oz Cointreau (or triple sec)
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Sugar for rim

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Rim martini glass with sugar (lemon wedge + sugar plate)
2. Shake all ingredients hard 10-15 seconds
3. Strain into sugar-rimmed martini glass

**Garnish:** Lemon twist or wheel

**Variations:**
- **Berry Lemon Drop** → Muddle raspberries or strawberries
- **Lavender Lemon Drop** → Use lavender simple syrup

**Glass:** Martini

---

### 🍹 LONG ISLAND ICED TEA | Sour & Bright
**Dangerously Strong, Deceptively Smooth**

**Ingredients:**
- 0.5 oz vodka
- 0.5 oz white rum
- 0.5 oz gin
- 0.5 oz blanco tequila
- 0.5 oz Cointreau (or triple sec)
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Splash of cola (for color)

**Method:** SHAKE + BUILD
1. Shake all ingredients except cola hard 10-15 seconds
2. Strain into Collins glass filled with ice
3. Top with small splash of cola (for iced tea color)
4. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Lemon wedge

**Variations:**
- **Texas Tea** → Add bourbon alongside other spirits
- **Blue Long Island** → Use blue curaçao instead of Cointreau, lemon-lime soda instead of cola
- **Long Beach Iced Tea** → Cranberry juice instead of cola

**Notes:** Despite the name, contains NO actual tea. Approximately 22% ABV - sip responsibly!

**Glass:** Collins or highball

### 🌅 TEQUILA SUNRISE | Sour & Bright
**Gradient Perfection**

![Tequila Sunrise](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/twenty-nine/Tequila_sunrise.jpg/320px-Tequila_sunrise.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz tequila blanco (silver tequila)
- 4 oz fresh orange juice
- 0.5 oz grenadine

**Method:** BUILD (Do NOT shake)
1. Fill highball glass with ice
2. Add tequila and orange juice
3. Stir to combine
4. Slowly pour grenadine down the side of glass or over back of spoon
5. Let grenadine sink to bottom naturally (DO NOT STIR)

**Garnish:** Orange slice + maraschino cherry

**Variations:**
- **Tequila Sunset** → Use grapefruit juice instead of orange, or add crème de cassis
- **Vodka Sunrise** → Swap tequila for vodka
- **Spicy Sunrise** → Infuse tequila with jalapeño overnight

**Notes:** The grenadine sinks to create the signature "sunrise" gradient effect

**Glass:** Highball or Collins

### 🍷 SANGRIA | Sour & Bright
**Spanish Wine Punch**

**Ingredients (Serves 4-6):**
- 1 bottle (750ml) Spanish red wine (Tempranillo, Garnacha, or Rioja)
- 2 oz brandy (or orange liqueur like Cointreau)
- 1/4 cup fresh orange juice
- 2 tbsp sugar (or simple syrup to taste)
- 1 orange, sliced
- 1 apple, chopped
- 1 lemon, sliced
- Optional: cinnamon stick, berries
- Optional: club soda for fizz

**Method:** MUDDLE + CHILL
1. In large pitcher, combine wine, brandy, orange juice, and sugar
2. Stir until sugar dissolves
3. Add sliced fruit and cinnamon stick
4. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (or overnight for best flavor)
5. Serve over ice in wine glasses
6. Optional: top with splash of club soda right before serving

**Garnish:** Orange slice

**Variations:**
- **White Sangria** → Use white wine (Albariño, Sauvignon Blanc) and lighter fruits (peaches, citrus)
- **Rosé Sangria** → Use rosé wine
- **Sparkling Sangria** → Top with Prosecco or cava

**Notes:** Sangria improves with time - 8-24 hours chill time is ideal. Use sturdy fruits that won't get mushy (apples, citrus, pears).

**Glass:** Wine glass or rocks

## SWEET & EASY (Crowd-Pleasers)

### 🍹 PIÑA COLADA | Sweet & Easy
**Tropical Paradise**

![Piña Colada](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/thirty-six/Pina_Colada_with_Pineapple_Garnish.jpg/320px-Pina_Colada_with_Pineapple_Garnish.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz white rum
- 2 oz pineapple juice
- 1.5 oz cream of coconut (Coco López)

**Method:** BLEND (or SHAKE)
1. Blend all ingredients with 1-1.5 cups ice until smooth
2. OR shake with ice and strain over fresh ice

**Garnish:** Pineapple wedge + cherry

**Variations:**
- **Virgin Piña Colada** → Skip the rum
- **Amaretto Colada** → Amaretto instead of rum

**Glass:** Hurricane or Collins

---

### 🥛 WHITE RUSSIAN | Sweet & Easy
**The Big Lebowski's Favorite**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz vodka
- 1 oz Kahlúa (coffee liqueur)
- 1 oz heavy cream (or half-and-half)

**Method:** BUILD
1. Add vodka and Kahlúa to rocks glass over ice
2. Stir briefly
3. Float cream on top (or stir in for creamy version)

**Garnish:** None

**Variations:**
- **Black Russian** → No cream
- **Mudslide** → Add Irish cream, blend with ice

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned

---

### 🍹 MAI TAI | Sweet & Easy
**Tiki Classic**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz aged rum (or 1 oz light + 1 oz dark)
- 0.75 oz Cointreau (or Curaçao)
- 0.75 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.5 oz orgeat (almond syrup)
- 0.25 oz simple syrup

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Shake all ingredients hard 10-15 seconds
2. Strain over crushed ice in rocks glass

**Garnish:** Mint sprig + lime wheel

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned or tiki mug

## LIGHT & REFRESHING (Low ABV)

### 🌿 MOJITO | Light & Refreshing
**Cuba's Refreshing Classic**

![Mojito](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5b/Mojito_cocktail.jpg/320px-Mojito_cocktail.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz white rum
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- 8-10 fresh mint leaves
- Club soda (2-3 oz)

**Method:** MUDDLE + BUILD
1. Gently muddle mint leaves and simple syrup in glass (don't shred!)
2. Add rum and lime juice
3. Fill glass with ice
4. Top with 2-3 oz club soda
5. Stir gently to combine

**Garnish:** Fresh mint sprig + lime wedge

**Glass:** Collins or highball

---

### 🍸 GIN & TONIC | Light & Refreshing
**The British Classic**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz gin
- 4-6 oz tonic water (Fever-Tree recommended)

**Method:** BUILD
1. Fill highball glass with ice
2. Add gin
3. Top with tonic water
4. Stir gently (1-2 stirs only)

**Garnish:** Lime wedge (squeezed and dropped in)

**Variations:**
- Use premium tonic
- Add cucumber, herbs, or berries

**Glass:** Highball

---

### 🍺 MOSCOW MULE | Light & Refreshing
**The Copper Mug Classic**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz vodka
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 4-6 oz ginger beer (NOT ginger ale)

**Method:** BUILD
1. Fill copper mug (or highball) with ice
2. Add vodka and lime juice
3. Top with ginger beer
4. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Lime wedge

**Variations:**
- **Dark & Stormy** → Dark rum instead of vodka (Gosling's traditional)
- **Kentucky Mule** → Bourbon instead of vodka
- **Mexican Mule** → Tequila instead of vodka

**Glass:** Copper mug or highball

---

### 🍋 TOM COLLINS | Light & Refreshing
**The Refreshing Gin Sour**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Club soda (2-3 oz)

**Method:** SHAKE + BUILD
1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice
2. Strain into Collins glass over fresh ice
3. Top with 2-3 oz club soda
4. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Lemon wheel + cherry

**Variations:**
- **Vodka Collins** → Vodka instead of gin
- **Whiskey Collins** → Bourbon instead of gin

**Glass:** Collins

---

### 🍊 PALOMA | Light & Refreshing
**Mexico's Most Popular Cocktail**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz tequila (100% agave blanco)
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- Grapefruit soda (Jarritos, Squirt) OR fresh grapefruit juice + club soda
- Optional: salt rim

**Method:** BUILD
1. Optional: salt rim (half only)
2. Fill highball glass with ice
3. Add tequila and lime juice
4. Top with grapefruit soda (or 2 oz grapefruit juice + 2-3 oz club soda)
5. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Grapefruit wedge

**Glass:** Highball

## BITTER & COMPLEX (Aperitifs)

### 🍊 APEROL SPRITZ | Bitter & Complex
**Italian Summer**

![Aperol Spritz](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f9/Aperol_Spritz_Cocktail.jpg/320px-Aperol_Spritz_Cocktail.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 3 oz Prosecco
- 2 oz Aperol

**Method:** BUILD
1. Fill wine glass with ice
2. Add ingredients in 3:2:1 ratio
3. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Orange slice

**Glass:** Wine glass or rocks

**Bitter Italian Aperitif**

**Ingredients:**
- 3 oz Prosecco
- 2 oz Campari
- 1 oz club soda

**Method:** BUILD
1. Same as Aperol Spritz (more bitter)

**Garnish:** Orange slice

**Glass:** Wine glass or rocks

---

### 🥃 SAZERAC | Bitter & Complex
**New Orleans' Official Cocktail**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz rye whiskey
- 0.25 oz simple syrup
- 3 dashes Peychaud's bitters
- Absinthe (for rinse)

**Method:** STIR + RINSE
1. Rinse chilled rocks glass with absinthe, dump excess
2. In mixing glass, stir rye, simple syrup, and bitters with ice 30-40s
3. Strain into absinthe-rinsed glass (no ice)
4. Express lemon peel over drink, discard peel (don't drop in)

**Garnish:** Lemon peel (expressed, discarded)

**Glass:** Rocks/Old Fashioned

## RICH & CREAMY (Dessert Drinks)

### ☕ ESPRESSO MARTINI | Rich & Creamy
**Caffeinated Elegance**

![Espresso Martini](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c5/Espresso_martini_cocktail.jpg/320px-Espresso_martini_cocktail.jpg)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz vodka
- 1 oz Kahlúa (coffee liqueur)
- 1 oz fresh espresso (cooled to room temp)
- 0.25 oz simple syrup

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Cool espresso to room temperature first
2. Shake HARD 15-20 seconds (creates thick foam)
3. Strain into chilled martini glass

**Garnish:** 3 coffee beans on foam

**Glass:** Martini or coupe

---

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Shake all ingredients hard 10-15 seconds
2. Strain into chilled coupe

**Glass:** Coupe

## Recipe Notes

### The Golden Ratios

**Classic Sour:**
- 2 oz spirit
- 0.75 oz citrus
- 0.75 oz sweetener

**Daiquiri/Margarita Family:**
- 2 oz spirit
- 1 oz citrus
- 0.75 oz sweetener

**Equal Parts:**
- Negroni: 1:1:1
- Last Word: 0.75:0.75:0.75:0.75

**Martini Range:**
- Classic: 2.5:0.5 (5:1)
- Dry: 6:1 or just a rinse
- Wet: 2:1
- 50/50: Equal parts

---

### Adjusting to Taste

**Too sweet?** → Add more citrus or bitters  
**Too sour?** → Add more simple syrup  
**Too strong?** → Add more mixer or dilute slightly  
**Too weak?** → Add more base spirit  
**Too bitter?** → Add simple syrup or reduce Campari/bitters

---

*Remember: These are templates. Once you master them, adjust to your taste.*
