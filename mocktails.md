## What Are Mocktails?

Mocktails are non-alcoholic cocktails that deliver the same complexity, flavor, and presentation as traditional cocktails—without the alcohol. Perfect for:
- Designated drivers
- Pregnancy
- Dry January participants
- Anyone choosing mindful drinking
- All-ages gatherings
- Health-conscious individuals

**The golden rule:** A great mocktail balances sweet, sour, and bitter just like a cocktail, and never relies solely on sugar and juice.

## Classic Mocktails

### 🍒 SHIRLEY TEMPLE
**The Original Kid's Cocktail**

**Ingredients:**
- 6 oz ginger ale (or lemon-lime soda)
- 0.5 oz grenadine
- Maraschino cherries

**Method:** BUILD
1. Fill glass with ice
2. Add ginger ale
3. Pour grenadine down the side (creates layered effect)
4. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Maraschino cherry (2-3)

**Variations:**
- **Dirty Shirley** → Add vodka (makes it a cocktail)
- **Roy Rogers** → Use cola instead of ginger ale

**Glass:** Highball

---

### 🌿 VIRGIN MOJITO
**Minty, Fresh, Refreshing**

**Ingredients:**
- 10-12 fresh mint leaves
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- Club soda (4-5 oz)

**Method:** MUDDLE + BUILD
1. Gently muddle mint leaves and simple syrup in glass
2. Add lime juice and ice
3. Top with club soda
4. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Fresh mint sprig + lime wedge

**Glass:** Highball or Collins

---

### 🥥 VIRGIN PIÑA COLADA
**Tropical Paradise**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz pineapple juice
- 1.5 oz cream of coconut (Coco López)
- 1 oz heavy cream (or coconut cream)

**Method:** BLEND
1. Blend all ingredients with 1-1.5 cups ice until smooth
2. Pour into glass

**Garnish:** Pineapple wedge + cherry

**Glass:** Hurricane or poco glass

---

### 🍋 VIRGIN LEMONADE SPARKLER
**Simple & Classic**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1 oz simple syrup (or honey)
- 4 oz club soda or sparkling water

**Method:** SHAKE + BUILD
1. Shake lemon juice and simple syrup with ice
2. Strain into glass filled with ice
3. Top with club soda

**Garnish:** Lemon wheel

**Variations:**
- **Strawberry Lemonade** → Add fresh strawberries
- **Lavender Lemonade** → Use lavender simple syrup
- **Blueberry Lemonade** → Muddle blueberries

**Glass:** Highball

## Fruity & Refreshing

### 🍓 STRAWBERRY BASIL SMASH
**Garden Fresh**

**Ingredients:**
- 4-5 fresh strawberries
- 4-5 fresh basil leaves
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- Club soda (2-3 oz)

**Method:** MUDDLE + SHAKE
1. Muddle strawberries and basil in shaker
2. Add lemon juice, simple syrup, and ice
3. Shake hard 10 seconds
4. Strain into glass over fresh ice
5. Top with club soda

**Garnish:** Strawberry slice + basil leaf

**Glass:** Rocks or highball

---

### 🍉 WATERMELON MINT COOLER
**Summer Refresher**

**Ingredients:**
- 4 oz fresh watermelon juice (blended watermelon, strained)
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.5 oz agave syrup
- 6-8 mint leaves
- Sparkling water (2 oz)

**Method:** MUDDLE + BUILD
1. Gently muddle mint leaves in glass
2. Add watermelon juice, lime juice, and agave
3. Fill with ice
4. Top with sparkling water
5. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Watermelon wedge + mint sprig

**Glass:** Highball

---

### 🥭 TROPICAL SUNRISE
**Island Vibes**

**Ingredients:**
- 3 oz orange juice
- 2 oz pineapple juice
- 0.5 oz grenadine
- Splash of coconut water

**Method:** BUILD
1. Fill glass with ice
2. Add orange juice, pineapple juice, and coconut water
3. Stir to combine
4. Slowly pour grenadine to create sunrise effect (do not stir)

**Garnish:** Orange slice + cherry

**Glass:** Highball

---

### 🍑 PEACH ICED TEA
**Southern Sweetness**

**Ingredients:**
- 4 oz brewed black tea (chilled)
- 2 oz peach nectar or fresh peach puree
- 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.5 oz honey or simple syrup

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Shake all ingredients with ice
2. Strain into glass over fresh ice

**Garnish:** Peach slice + lemon wheel

**Glass:** Highball or mason jar

## Sophisticated Sips

### 🍊 CITRUS ROSEMARY SPRITZ
**Herbaceous & Elegant**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz fresh grapefruit juice
- 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice
- 0.5 oz rosemary simple syrup
- 3 oz club soda
- Fresh rosemary sprig

**Method:** BUILD
1. Fill wine glass with ice
2. Add juices and rosemary syrup
3. Top with club soda
4. Gently stir

**Garnish:** Rosemary sprig + grapefruit slice

**Glass:** Wine glass or highball

---

### 🫐 BLUEBERRY THYME LEMONADE
**Garden Party Perfect**

**Ingredients:**
- 6-8 fresh blueberries
- 2-3 fresh thyme sprigs
- 1.5 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1 oz simple syrup
- 3 oz sparkling water

**Method:** MUDDLE + SHAKE
1. Muddle blueberries and thyme in shaker
2. Add lemon juice, simple syrup, and ice
3. Shake hard 10 seconds
4. Double strain into glass over ice
5. Top with sparkling water

**Garnish:** Blueberries + thyme sprig

**Glass:** Coupe or rocks

---

### 🌸 CUCUMBER ELDERFLOWER FIZZ
**Light & Floral**

**Ingredients:**
- 3-4 cucumber slices
- 1 oz elderflower syrup
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 4 oz sparkling water or tonic

**Method:** MUDDLE + BUILD
1. Muddle cucumber slices in glass
2. Add elderflower syrup and lime juice
3. Fill with ice
4. Top with sparkling water
5. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Cucumber ribbon + edible flowers (optional)

**Glass:** Highball or wine glass

---

### ☕ VIRGIN ESPRESSO MARTINI
**Coffee Kick**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz fresh espresso (cooled)
- 1 oz simple syrup (or coffee syrup)
- 0.5 oz heavy cream (optional for creamier version)

**Method:** SHAKE
1. Cool espresso to room temperature
2. Shake all ingredients HARD with ice for 15-20 seconds
3. Strain into chilled martini glass

**Garnish:** 3 coffee beans on foam

**Variations:**
- Add 0.5 oz chocolate syrup for mocha version
- Use cold brew instead of espresso

**Glass:** Martini or coupe

## Party Punches

### 🍓 BERRY LEMONADE PUNCH
**Serves 8-10**

**Ingredients:**
- 4 cups lemonade (fresh or high-quality store-bought)
- 2 cups mixed berry juice
- 1 cup fresh strawberries, sliced
- 1 cup fresh blueberries
- 1 cup fresh raspberries
- 2 cups ginger ale or club soda
- Fresh mint leaves

**Method:** BUILD
1. In large punch bowl, combine lemonade and berry juice
2. Add sliced strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries
3. Refrigerate for 1-2 hours
4. Just before serving, add ginger ale and ice
5. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Mint sprigs + berry skewers

---

### 🎄 CHRISTMAS MORNING PUNCH
**Serves 8-10**

**Ingredients:**
- 3 cups pomegranate juice
- 2 cups orange juice
- 1 cup pineapple juice
- 2 cups ginger ale
- Pomegranate seeds for garnish
- Orange slices

**Method:** BUILD
1. Combine all juices in punch bowl
2. Add ice or freeze juice into ice cubes (prevents dilution)
3. Just before serving, add ginger ale
4. Stir gently

**Garnish:** Pomegranate seeds + orange slices

---

### 🍹 TROPICAL FRUIT PUNCH
**Serves 6-8**

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups pineapple juice
- 1 cup orange juice
- 1 cup mango juice
- 1 cup coconut water
- 1 cup club soda
- Fresh pineapple chunks
- Maraschino cherries

**Method:** BUILD
1. Combine all juices and coconut water in pitcher
2. Refrigerate until chilled
3. Add club soda just before serving
4. Serve over ice

**Garnish:** Pineapple chunks + cherries

## Mocktail Tips & Techniques

### Building Better Mocktails

**Balance is Key:**
- Sweet (simple syrup, honey, agave)
- Sour (citrus juices, vinegar-based shrubs)
- Bitter (tonic water, bitters like Angostura*)
- Aromatic (herbs, spices, fruit)

*Note: Most bitters contain trace alcohol (<0.5% ABV), similar to vanilla extract

**Fresh Ingredients Matter:**
- Always use fresh citrus juice (never bottled)
- Fresh herbs add complexity
- Real fruit purees beat artificial syrups

**Carbonation Adds Life:**
- Club soda, sparkling water, tonic, ginger beer
- Add sparkling elements last (prevents going flat)
- Don't over-stir carbonated drinks

**Ice Quality:**
- Use fresh, clean ice
- Large cubes melt slower (less dilution)
- Crushed ice is great for slushies and tiki-style drinks

- Tonic water

**Fresh Ingredients:**
- Lemons, limes, oranges, grapefruit

- Seasonal fruits

**Specialty Items:**
- Coconut cream
- Ginger (fresh)
- Bitters (optional, trace alcohol)

### Non-Alcoholic Spirit Alternatives

For more complex mocktails, consider:
- **Seedlip** (non-alcoholic distilled spirits)
- **Lyre's** (non-alcoholic spirit alternatives)
- **Ritual Zero Proof** (whiskey, gin, tequila alternatives)
- **Monday Zero Alcohol Gin**

These products contain <0.5% ABV and provide botanical complexity without alcohol.

---

### Garnishing Mocktails

**Make it Look Special:**
- Fresh herb sprigs (mint, rosemary, basil)
- Citrus wheels, wedges, or twists
- Fresh berries
- Edible flowers
- Cucumber ribbons
- Sugared or salted rims
- Fun straws, cocktail picks, umbrellas

**Pro Tip:** Presentation matters! Use nice glassware, add ice, and garnish thoughtfully. A well-presented mocktail feels just as special as a cocktail.
