## Old Fashioneds

### Classic Old Fashioned
- 2 oz Buffalo Trace
- 3 Dash Angostura Bitters
- .25 oz Turbinado Simple
- Fat Ice Cube
- **Garnish:** Orange Peel & Luxardo Cherry
- **Glass:** Old Fashioned

Build in glass. Add whiskey, bitters, simple then Fat Ice Cube, stir in glass with bar spoon. Add orange peel & Luxardo Cherry. DO NOT muddle fruit. Do not build in mixing glass with ice, then strain into glass — this dilutes the cocktail.

## Margaritas

### The OG Margarita
- 1.5 oz Hornitos Blanco
- .75 oz Cointreau
- .75 oz Lime Juice
- Salt or Tajin Rim Optional
- **Garnish:** Lime Wedge
- **Glass:** 9 oz Highball (rocks)

In a shaker with ice, combine all ingredients, shake well. Strain into glass with fresh ice. Garnish with lime wedge.
