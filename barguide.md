## Bar Essentials

### Core Spirits (The Essential Six)
- **Vodka** → Martinis, Cosmos, Moscow Mules, White Russians
- **Gin** → Martinis, Negronis, G&Ts, Tom Collins
- **White Rum** → Mojitos, Daiquiris, Piña Coladas
- **Tequila (Blanco)** → Margaritas, Palomas (100% agave only)
- **Whiskey** → Old Fashioneds, Manhattans, Whiskey Sours
- **Brandy/Cognac** → Sidecars, Brandy Alexanders

### Key Modifiers
- **Dry Vermouth** → Martinis (refrigerate after opening, use within 1-2 months)
- **Sweet Vermouth** → Manhattans, Negronis (refrigerate)
- **Orange Liqueur** → Margaritas, Sidecars (Cointreau > Triple Sec)
- **Coffee Liqueur** → White Russians, Espresso Martinis (Kahlúa standard)
- **Campari** → Negronis (bitter, acquired taste)
- **Aperol** → Aperol Spritzes (lighter, sweeter than Campari)

### Mixers
- **Fresh Citrus** → Lemons, limes, oranges (NEVER bottled juice)
- **Simple Syrup** → 1:1 sugar:water, dissolved and cooled
- **Club Soda** → Unflavored carbonated water
- **Tonic Water** → Bitter, sweetened (Fever-Tree recommended)
- **Ginger Beer** → NOT ginger ale (spicy, strong flavor)
- **Bitters** → Angostura (aromatic), Orange (citrusy)

### Tools
- **Shaker** → Boston (pro) or Cobbler (beginner-friendly)
- **Jigger** → 1 oz / 2 oz or 0.75 oz / 1.5 oz measuring tool
- **Bar Spoon** → Long-handled for stirring
- **Strainer** → Hawthorne (for shakers) or Julep (for mixing glass)
- **Muddler** → For crushing herbs/fruit
- **Citrus Juicer** → Essential for fresh juice
- **Peeler** → For citrus twists

## Technique Reference

### The Five Fundamentals

**SHAKE** → Citrus, juice, cream, egg whites, syrups  
- Fill shaker 3/4 with ice, seal, shake hard 10-15 seconds
- Sound changes from "crunchy" to "wet" when ready
- Examples: Margarita, Daiquiri, Whiskey Sour, Cosmo

**STIR** → Spirit-only cocktails (no juice/cream/citrus)  
- Add ice to mixing glass, pour spirits, stir 30-40 seconds
- Stir until glass exterior is frosty
- Examples: Martini, Manhattan, Negroni, Old Fashioned

**BUILD** → Simple highballs  
- Ice in glass → spirit → mixer → quick stir
- Examples: G&T, Rum & Coke, Moscow Mule

**MUDDLE** → Extract oils/flavor from herbs or fruit  
- Herbs: Gentle pressure, twist, release oils (don't shred)
- Fruit: More aggressive to break down
- Examples: Mojito, Mint Julep, Old Fashioned (sugar method)

**BLEND** → Frozen drinks  
- Spirits + mixers + ice, blend until smooth
- Use crushed ice if available
- Examples: Frozen Margarita, Piña Colada

### Measurements
- 1 dash = 1/8 tsp
- 1 barspoon = 1 tsp
- 1 oz = 2 tbsp = 30 ml
- 1 jigger = typically 1 oz / 2 oz sides

**The Golden Sour Ratio:** 2 oz spirit : 0.75 oz citrus : 0.75 oz sweet

## The Golden Rules

1. **Fresh citrus juice, always** → No bottled juice, ever
2. **Measure your ingredients** → Use a jigger until you're a pro
3. **Chill your glassware** → Freezer or ice water while mixing
4. **Stir stirred, shake shaken** → Spirit-only = stir; juice/citrus = shake
5. **Taste as you go** → Adjust sweetness, tartness, strength
6. **Simple garnishes** → Must add aroma, flavor, or visual appeal
7. **Make classics yours** → Learn the rules, then adapt to taste

## Pro Tips

### Ice Matters
- **Standard cubes:** Most drinks
- **Large cubes (2"):** Rocks drinks (slower melt, less dilution)
- **Crushed ice:** Mint Juleps, Tiki drinks (chills fast, melts fast)
- Fill shaker 3/4 full • Fresh ice for each drink • Never reuse

### Citrus
- Fresh juice lasts ~4 hours before oxidizing
- Always squeeze fresh, never use bottled
- Lemon for whiskey drinks, lime for rum/tequila

### Vermouth
- It's wine → refrigerate after opening
- Use within 1-2 months
- Old vermouth = ruined drink

### Garnishes
- **Twist:** Express oils over drink, rub rim, drop in or discard
- **Expressed:** Spray citrus oils over surface
- **Rim:** Citrus wedge + salt/sugar (rim half only to give choice)

### Common Ratios
- **Classic Sour:** 2 : 0.75 : 0.75 (spirit : citrus : sweet)
- **Daiquiri/Margarita family:** 2 : 1 : 0.75
- **Negroni:** 1 : 1 : 1 (equal parts)
- **Martini:** 5:1 to 2:1 (gin : vermouth, adjust to taste)

## Quick Reference Cards

### SHAKE vs STIR Decision Tree

**Does it contain ANY of these?**
- Citrus juice (lemon, lime, etc.)
- Fruit juice (cranberry, pineapple, etc.)
- Cream or dairy
- Egg white
- Simple syrup + citrus

**YES** → **SHAKE IT**

**NO** (spirits only) → **STIR IT**

### Dilution Guide

| Method | Dilution | Result |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Shake (10-15s)** | High | Cold, aerated, slightly watered |
| **Stir (30-40s)** | Medium | Cold, silky, minimal water |
| **Build** | Low | Simple, quick, less integrated |
| **Blend** | Very High | Smooth, frozen, heavily diluted |

---

### Temperature Guide

| Drink Type | Serving Temp | Method |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| **Shaken cocktails** | Ice cold | Shake until frost forms |
| **Stirred cocktails** | Chilled | Stir until glass frosts |
| **Highballs** | Cold | Build over fresh ice |
| **Neat spirits** | Room temp | No ice, room temp glass |
| **Frozen drinks** | Frozen | Blend until slushy |

---

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Too strong** | Under-diluted | Shake/stir longer, add more ice |
| **Too weak** | Over-diluted | Shake/stir less, use larger ice |
| **Too sweet** | Too much syrup | Add more citrus/bitters |
| **Too sour** | Too much citrus | Add more simple syrup |
| **Flat/watery** | Old ice or poor technique | Fresh ice, shake harder |
| **Cloudy (stirred drink)** | Shaken instead of stirred | Stir spirit-only drinks |

### Building Your Home Bar (Priority Order)

**LEVEL 1 - Basics** ($100-150)
- Vodka (Tito's, Smirnoff)
- Gin (Beefeater, Tanqueray)
- White Rum (Bacardi, Havana Club)
- Simple syrup (make it yourself)
- Fresh lemons & limes
- Basic shaker + jigger

**LEVEL 2 - Expanding** ($150-250 more)
- Tequila (Espolòn, Olmeca Altos)
- Bourbon (Buffalo Trace, Wild Turkey)
- Dry vermouth (Dolin)
- Sweet vermouth (Carpano Antica)
- Cointreau
- Angostura bitters
- Bar spoon + strainer

**LEVEL 3 - Complete** ($200-300 more)
- Premium spirits (upgrade your favorites)
- Campari
- Kahlúa
- Orange bitters
- Muddler
- Proper glassware
